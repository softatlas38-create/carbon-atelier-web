# Carbon Diamond Atelier

Build a premium, high-end automotive service website for Carbon Diamond Car, a luxury car customization, detailing and repair company based in Morocco.

I am attaching a screenshot of the company's current Instagram profile as the main visual reference for the brand identity. Use it to understand the existing logo, colors, automotive atmosphere and luxury positioning, but create a much more sophisticated and professional website than the Instagram profile.

MAIN GOAL

The website should immediately feel like a luxury automotive atelier, not a generic car garage website.

The main objective is to showcase the company's services in a visually impressive way and make visitors want to contact/book the company.

The design must communicate:

Luxury

Precision

Performance

Exclusivity

Automotive craftsmanship

Premium detailing

Trust

Modern Moroccan luxury business

The website should feel comparable to the digital experience of a high-end automotive brand or luxury car customization studio.

VISUAL DIRECTION

Create a cinematic, dark and premium interface.

Use:

Deep black / near-black background

Graphite and charcoal surfaces

White typography

Subtle metallic/gold accents inspired by the Carbon Diamond Car logo

Very subtle gradients

Glassmorphism only where appropriate

Large premium typography

Strong visual hierarchy

Generous spacing

Thin elegant borders

Soft shadows

High-quality automotive imagery

Smooth micro-interactions

Elegant reveal animations

Sophisticated hover effects

Subtle parallax effects

Avoid:

Generic SaaS UI

Bright blue/purple gradients

Excessive rounded cards

Cheap-looking animations

Overloaded pages

Stock-photo feeling

Excessive text

Template-like layouts

The final result must feel expensive, cinematic and exclusive.

WEBSITE STRUCTURE

Create a responsive multi-section homepage with the following structure:

1. HERO — CINEMATIC FIRST IMPRESSION

Full-screen hero section.

Use a dramatic luxury vehicle image/video background.

Add a dark cinematic overlay so the typography remains extremely readable.

Small eyebrow:
"CARBON DIAMOND CAR"

Main headline:

"YOUR CAR.
OUR CRAFT."

Alternative French headline:
"L'EXCELLENCE AUTOMOBILE,
REPENSÉE."

Supporting text:

"Préparation, protection et personnalisation automobile avec une exigence sans compromis."

Primary CTA:
"Découvrir nos services"

Secondary CTA:
"Nous contacter"

Add a subtle scroll indicator at the bottom.

The hero should feel like the opening scene of a premium automotive commercial.

2. BRAND INTRO

Create a minimal editorial section introducing Carbon Diamond Car.

Headline:

"Plus qu'un service automobile.
Une signature."

Short paragraph explaining that Carbon Diamond Car combines expertise, precision and premium finishing to transform and protect exceptional vehicles.

Do not make this section text-heavy.

Use an elegant split layout with an automotive workshop image.

3. SERVICES — THE MOST IMPORTANT SECTION

Create a visually impressive services section.

Title:

"NOS SERVICES"

Subtitle:

"Chaque détail compte."

Display the company's services as large premium interactive cards or editorial panels.

Services:

HABILAGE
Interior/exterior automotive customization and finishing.

DIAGNOSTIQUE
Professional vehicle diagnostics and technical inspection.

TÔLERIE
Bodywork repair and precision restoration.

LISSAGE / POLISSAGE
Professional surface correction, polishing and finishing.

LAVAGE / PPF
Premium cleaning, paint protection film and vehicle protection.

JANTES
Wheel customization, restoration and finishing.

TEINTURE DES VITRES
Premium window tinting for style, privacy and comfort.

IMPORTANT:

Do NOT create a boring 7-card grid.

Make the service presentation editorial and luxurious.

For example:

Large image

Service number

Service name

Short description

Arrow / "Découvrir"

Elegant hover animation

Image zoom or reveal interaction

On desktop, create an asymmetric editorial composition.

On mobile, transform it into a beautiful vertical experience.

4. FEATURED WORK / SHOWCASE

Create a cinematic gallery titled:

"NOTRE SAVOIR-FAIRE"

Showcase premium vehicles and completed work.

Use large images rather than small thumbnail grids.

Create categories/filters:

TOUS
HABILLAGE
POLISSAGE
PPF
JANTES
TÔLERIE
DETAILING

Images should have elegant hover effects.

Clicking an image should open a premium fullscreen lightbox.

5. BEFORE / AFTER

Create a highly visual before/after section.

Title:

"LA TRANSFORMATION SE VOIT."

Use an interactive before/after slider showing vehicle transformations.

Make the slider smooth and premium.

This section should be visually striking.

6. WHY CARBON DIAMOND CAR

Create a premium section explaining why customers choose the company.

Use 4 minimalist principles:

01 — PRÉCISION
Chaque finition est réalisée avec une attention particulière.

02 — EXPERTISE
Un savoir-faire dédié à l'automobile premium.

03 — EXIGENCE
Nous ne cherchons pas simplement le résultat. Nous cherchons le résultat parfait.

04 — SIGNATURE
Chaque véhicule doit ressortir avec une identité unique.

Use large typography and minimal visual elements.

7. PREMIUM VEHICLE SHOWCASE

Create a full-width cinematic section featuring an exceptional luxury vehicle.

Overlay:

"VOTRE VÉHICULE.
VOTRE SIGNATURE."

CTA:
"Parlons de votre projet"

8. CONTACT / BOOKING

Create a premium contact section.

Headline:

"PRÊT À TRANSFORMER VOTRE VÉHICULE ?"

Include:

Name

Phone

Email

Vehicle

Service selector

Message

Primary CTA:
"Demander un devis"

Also prominently display WhatsApp/contact option.

Use the company's current contact information from the attached reference where appropriate, but do not invent additional information.

Minimal luxury footer.

Include:

"Automotive Excellence."

Accueil
Services

Use a premium modern sans-serif typography system.

Recommended:

Inter

Manrope

or another sophisticated geometric sans-serif

Use very large headlines with tight letter spacing.

Mix uppercase labels with elegant sentence-case body text.

Typography should feel editorial and automotive-luxury.

ANIMATIONS

Animations must be subtle and premium.

Use:

Fade-up reveals

Image scale on hover

Smooth page transitions

Magnetic CTA effect

Staggered service reveals

Scroll-based image movement

Smooth navbar transition

Before/after slider interaction

Elegant cursor interactions on desktop

Do NOT over-animate the website.

The animations should make the website feel expensive, not like a gaming website.

RESPONSIVE DESIGN

The website must be exceptional on:

Mobile

Tablet

Desktop

Large desktop screens

Mobile is extremely important.

Do not simply shrink the desktop layout.

Create intentional mobile layouts with:

Excellent spacing

Large readable typography

Easy thumb-friendly buttons

Horizontal image galleries where appropriate

Smooth navigation

Fast-loading imagery

UX

The visitor journey should be:

HERO
→ SERVICES
→ SHOWCASE
→ TRANSFORMATION
→ WHY US
→ CONTACT

Every important section should naturally lead toward contacting the company.

Use clear CTAs throughout the website.

TECHNICAL / QUALITY REQUIREMENTS

Build a clean, production-quality React website.

Use reusable components.

Keep the design system consistent across the entire website.

Use semantic HTML.

Optimize images and loading performance.

Add SEO-friendly page title and metadata.

Make all buttons and navigation links functional.

Do not use lorem ipsum.

Use realistic French/French-Moroccan automotive copy.

Do not invent fake customer reviews, fake awards, fake statistics or fake company history.

If information is missing, keep the section minimal rather than inventing facts.

VERY IMPORTANT DESIGN RULE

Do not create a generic "AI-generated business website".

I want the final interface to look like a luxury automotive brand website, with an art-directed, cinematic and editorial design.

Think:

Porsche × Brabus × luxury detailing atelier × modern Moroccan premium brand.

The website should make someone visiting for the first time think:

"This company works with expensive cars and takes every detail seriously."

Before implementing anything, analyze the attached Instagram screenshot and use its existing visual identity as the starting point.

Start by creating the complete visual foundation and homepage experience first. Make the homepage visually exceptional before adding secondary functionality.

Ask me any questions you need in order to fully understand the brand and desired experience before making assumptions.
